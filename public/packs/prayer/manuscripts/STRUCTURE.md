## Working title
**What Actually Happens When You Pray** (family option: *Prayer, Actually Explained*)

## Thesis options (owner picks)
A. Prayer is not talking *to* someone over there — every serious tradition
   describes it as something happening *here* (transfer, identification,
   descent, remembrance). The distance prayer assumes is what prayer deletes.
B. Prayer is a technology with competing operating theories — the film is a
   tour of the mechanisms, same recipe as rebirth v1.
C. The honest version: nobody knows if asking changes outcomes — but the
   traditions were never primarily asking. (Guard-forward cut.)

## The seam (cold open material, owner words needed)
Everybody prays. Nobody agrees what it does: hotline to heaven? Self-talk
with incense? A technology that works whether or not anyone listens? The
film's question: what if the interesting traditions all deny the premise —
that prayer is one-directional talking to someone elsewhere?

## Shared ontology (v0 sketch — the table the film earns)

  folk frame, like the flame simile was for rebirth.
- Common spine (to test against each stop at draft): attention directed →
  distance collapses → identity shifts (transfer/identification/descent/
  remembrance are all distance-deletions).

  heirs), the unanswered-prayer problem (theodicy's hardest case), the
