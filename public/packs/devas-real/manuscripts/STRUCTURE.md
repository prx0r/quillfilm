## Working title
**Are Devas Real?** (family option: *Devas, Actually Explained*)

## Thesis
The word everyone half-knows — "angel" — is exactly the wrong word.
Devas are subject to death, not creators, not omniscient, not morally
perfect, not refuge-worthy. The question "are they real?" splits into
two questions the way jhana's did: real *as experiences the canon
describes*, or real *as external objects*? The film runs both.
