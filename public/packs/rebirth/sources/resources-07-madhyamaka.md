# RESOURCE — MADHYAMAKA (dissolution school)

## Mechanism
Rebirth with no one reborn and nothing traveling: all dharmas empty of
svabhāva, including nirvāṇa and emptiness itself. Conventionally karma works
and births happen; ultimately nothing stands alone long enough to be pinned.

## Metaphysics (analytic)
Two truths (saṃvṛti/paramārtha) are not two worlds but two registers of
talk. The tetralemma (catuṣkoṭi) removes every inventory-answer to "what
after death?" — the question assumes a findable Tathāgata and analysis finds
none (in aggregates, apart from them, as their owner). Dependent origination
IS emptiness (not a consequence of it): the causal series has no ground —
which is why "designation-on-conditions" (prajñapti) is the honest name for
what continues. Charitable to Theravāda continuism; hostile to every
substantial carrier, including ālaya-as-thing.

## Best objections
- If everything is empty, does karma have force? (Standard answer: emptiness
  is WHY causality works — no svabhāva needed for conditioning. State the charge
  first: the eternalism/nihilism double-bind the school lives inside.)
- Hyde 2021 on Bergson applies here too: extreme-dualism accusations miss
  the two-truths move; but Western readers will hear nihilism — quote the
  best opponent (Yamaka's own starting view!).
