# RESOURCE — YOGĀCĀRA (storehouse school)

## Mechanism
ālaya-vijñāna carries karmic seeds (bīja) perfumed by impressions (vāsanā).
Death: active senses withdraw; store withdraws carrying seed-account; ripe
seed sprouts as new birth-consciousness at conception. Path: transform the
store (śuddha-ālaya) rather than destroy a self.

## Metaphysics (analytic)
No ātman; continuity problem solved by a *subliminal stream* integrated into
Abhidharma momentariness (Waldron: the Ālaya Treatise's core contribution).
Consequence: consciousness-first ontology without a person — the store is
neither self nor not-self, it is the *medium* of karmic efficacy. Seeds are
prajñaptisat (nominal) per Sautrāntika inheritance — a metaphor promoted
to mechanism. Close cousin to Bhavanga (see Theravāda) and to Bergson's
past-conserving plane (SCIENCE file, not v1).

## Quotes (verbatim, cite)
- Sthiramati/Ālaya Treatise via Waldron 2003: "Ālayavijñāna functions as the
  condition (pratyaya) for [the forms of] active cognitive awareness in two
  ways: by being their seed (bījabhāva), and by providing their support
  (āśrayakara)." And: active awareness "nurtur[es] the seeds in this life…
  and caus[es] ālayavijñāna to persist on into the next life."
- Britannica: the store is "the seed-bed of all that exists… the final basis
  of the apparent individual"; vasanas = "perfumings."
- SEP (Szanyi): development "traced back specifically to… nirodhasamāpatti"
  — the cessation-attainment problem that forced a subliminal layer.
- Laṅkāvatāra (verify passage at draft): ālaya named; tathāgatagarbha
  identity — the Huayan upgrade card (Hamar 2023: one-mind with tathatā +
  saṃsāra doors).

## Best objections
- Reifies a quasi-substance (the Sautrāntika critique of Sarvāstivāda's
  prāpti is inherited and turned on ālaya itself: a metaphor smuggled in).
- Theravāda: unnecessary machinery — bhavanga does the job with less.
