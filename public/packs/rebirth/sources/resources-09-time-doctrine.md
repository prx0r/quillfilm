# RESOURCE — TIME DOCTRINE (kṣaṇa / santāna / Bergson)

## The claim
Buddhist time-doctrine is the hidden spine of "rebirth is constantly
happening." Two technical terms carry it:
- **kṣaṇa** — moment. NOT the pointillist caricature: the kṣaṇavāda
  tradition distinguishes momentary sensation from constructive perception
  (saṃjñā) and locates continuity in the stream (santāna) — Jung 2026
  (Phil East West, Oct): Husserl's living present and the kṣaṇa share the
  refusal of isolated now-points.
- **santāna** — the causal stream. Continuity without a wire: the reeds
  image is a santāna-image.

## Bergson in the same room (card-only in v1, full in v2-science)
- Duration-as-melody (Trumbull Hfield seminar, verbatim in library): "the
  indivisible and indestructible continuity of a melody where the past
  enters into the present and forms with it an undivided whole."
- Kumar 2024 (Sophia): Buddhaghosa's temporality through Bergson's duration.
- Bunnag: duration ENHANCES the causal chain (karma defense).
- The 1921 Chinese debate: intuitionism/élan ≈ ālayavijñāna (Yaobinbin).
