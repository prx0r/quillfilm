# RESOURCE — THERAVĀDA (Abhidhamma mechanics)

## Mechanism
Cuti-citta (death, 17th-moment sequence: bhavanga vibrates, 5 weak javanas
take kamma/kamma-nimitta/gati-nimitta) → paṭisandhi-citta arises in the next
existence on the same object. No gap, no traveller: one santāna. Conception =
three things (parents + season + gandhabba).

## Metaphysics (analytic)
Continuism without a self: the stream is a causal series of momentary cittas
sharing an object at the cut. The "link" is not an entity but an object-
continuity — the philosophical point is that *identity of object* does the
work identity-of-thing does elsewhere. Death-moment javanas lack reproductive
power; what ripens is the kamma they regulate — so the moral agent is the
series, not a person. Layers: Canon (MN 38, SN 22/44) vs commentarial
Abhidhamma elaboration (Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha §§11–13, Visuddhimagga XIII).

## Quotes (verbatim, cite — full texts in quotes/*.txt)
- MN 38 (gandhabba): "the descent of the embryo occurs with the union of
  three things… mother & father… her season… a gandhabba is present."
- Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha §12: "Immediately after [death-consciousness] has
  ceased, a rebirth consciousness arises and is established in the subsequent
  existence, based on the object thus obtained… That rebirth (or relinking)
  consciousness, so called because it links together the two consecutive
  existences."
- §13 footnote: "There is a misconception… that the subsequent birth is
  conditioned by this last decease-thought. What actually conditions rebirth
  is not this decease-thought… but that which is experienced during the
  javana process."
- Visuddhimagga XIII (Ñāṇamoli trans., in library, 853pp): the
  recollection-of-past-lives method (retrace to rebirth-linking, past
  death-moment as object) — the verification technique, v2-science lane.
