# RESOURCE — TIBETAN (bardo school)

## Mechanism
Five-element dissolution at death → Ground Luminosity (bde gshegs / zhi'i 'od gsal) →
miss → bardo of reality (42 peaceful + 58 wrathful deities) → bardo of becoming,
karma-winds to rebirth, ≤49 days. Tukdam = resting post-mortem in the luminosity.

## Metaphysics (analytic)
Two-tier ontology: conventional mind-stream (rnam rgyud) + luminous ground
that is not a soul but the basis of the stream. Rebirth = ground-stream
continuity under karmic imprint (bag chags). Deities in the bardo = one's
own mind-appearances (self-cognition rendered as theophany). The doctrine
is self-expressively sva-saṃvitti-virodha-resolving: no permanent self
assumed — only basis-continuity. Distinct from Yogācāra: no seed-store;
the *ground* luminosity does the carrying, imprints ride it.

  hearing frame — "Liberation in the Intermediate State through Hearing."
  Death-bardo: "at the end of a process of sensory dissolution… a profound
  state of consciousness, called the clear light, dawns. If one is able to
  recognize the clear light as reality, one immediately achieves liberation

- Dzigar Kongtrul Rinpoche (Rubin Museum): "the word for body is lu, which
  literally means 'what's left behind.' What we do not leave behind is our
  consciousness, which travels onward through the bardo."
