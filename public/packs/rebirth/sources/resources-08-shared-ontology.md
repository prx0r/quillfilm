# RESOURCE — SHARED ONTOLOGY (cross-school metaphysics table)

## The five axioms every school signs
| Axiom | Agreement | The disagreement is ONLY the carrier |
|---|---|---|
| No soul travels | anattā/anātman everywhere (incl. Vasubandhu's borrowed-term person) | continuum (Theravāda) · ground+luminosity (Tibetan) · seed-store (Yogācāra) · structure/vortex (Early) · vow (Pure Land) · no carrier (Madhyamaka) |
| Karma = momentum | acts perfume the future | javanas (Abhidhamma) · bag chags (Tibetan) · vāsanā (Yogācāra) · craving-clinging (Early) · ledger-cosigned-by-grace (Pure Land) · dependent designation (Madhyamaka) |
| Consciousness leads | mind-first, not matter-first | all six, in their own registers — this is the set's actual consensus against materialism |
| Nothing uncaused | paṭicca-samuppāda family | 12 links (Theravāda/Early) · 36 tattvas (Tibetan-adjacent Śaiva, out of v1) · 48 vows as meta-cause (Pure Land) |
| Verify > believe | test-it-yourself strand | recall-method (Visuddhimagga/Thai) · recognition (Early) · entrust-then-confirm (Pure Land) · deity-encounter as test (Tibetan) |

## The observer's line (film voice)
"They agree on everything except what carries it. Six answers to one
question: what keeps going? And not one of them says a soul."

## Best meta-objection
The table flatters agreement — a Pure Land devotee and a Madhyamikan would
fight the table itself. State it: the schools' *internal* disputes are
violent; the shared ontology is our compilation, not their creed.
