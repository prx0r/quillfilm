# RESOURCE — V2 SPINE: STREAM ENTRY ACTUALLY EXPLAINED

## The thesis
Sotāpatti is a *recognition*, not an acquisition: the removal of the error
of a permanent self (personality-view, sakkāya-diṭṭhi), the seeing of the
co-arising structure. It produces no new knowledge — it subtracts a
mis-perception. Ñāṇavīra's own record is the spine quote.
