# RESOURCE — THAI FOREST (kammatthāna branch)

## Mechanism
Theravāda mechanics + operational verification: pubbenivāsānussati checked by
teachers; adhiṭṭhāna (determination persisting past death — Mun's elephant);
iddhi as working tools. Rebirth as wager, not belief (apaṇṇaka safe-bet).

## Metaphysics (analytic)
No new ontology — the school's contribution is *epistemic*: rebirth as
working-hypothesis under the wager, confirmed by attainment. Structural
claim (Truth of Rebirth): DO's feedback loops make every factor implicitly
"re-"; without repeated birth the search for birth's causes (SN 12:65)
never happens. Position-4: phenomenological, refuses "what is reborn."

## Best objections
- The wager is pragmatic, not evidential — a modernist can accept the frame
  and reject the cosmology (the secular move the book exists to resist).
- Adhiṭṭhāna/Mun's elephant: unfalsifiable from outside the lineage.
