# STOP 5 — EGYPTIAN

## WHAT
No migration — ITERATION. The ka (vital double), ba (mobile soul-image),
akh (transfigured spirit) survive death; the ba commutes by day to the tomb
and by night joins the akh. The person becomes an "akh" — a justified
effective spirit — or dissolves. Rebirth is rare (the Osiris-identification
model: everyone *becomes* the god in death rather than getting another
life). The odd one out: an afterlife industry with almost NO conveyor belt.

## WHAT'S GOING ON
Death → the Opening of the Mouth rite (returns the senses to the mummy) →
Weighing of the Heart against Ma'at's feather (Thoth scribes the result) →
the Negative Confession (declarations of innocence) → if justified: the ba
rejoins the body/enters the Field of Reeds (Sekhet-Aaru), living forever
as one's "living one" — SILENCE AS THE AFTERLIFE: "the greatest thing is
silence" (Pyramid Texts — the famous refrain).

## THE EXACT MOMENT OF DEATH
The moment = the heart's weighing verdict + the Opening of the Mouth — the
moment the corpse's senses are ritually RESTORED (the dead continues
needing eyes/mouth: an afterlife with maintenance). For the film: "the
moment death happens, someone performs technology on your body so you can
keep living" — dead-life as engineering.

## LOGICAL ARGUMENT (verbatim bank, verify tr. at draft)
- **The Negative Confession (Papyrus of Ani, Spell 125)**: "I have not
  stolen. I have not killed. I have not caused pain…" — ~40 denials — the
  life-review as audit; the ancestor of every judgment scene.
- **Pyramid Texts**: "O king, you have not departed, you have gone" —
  death-refusal formula: even Egypt says the event-name is wrong ("you
  have not gone away").
- The Field of Reeds as agricultural-continuity model (labor continues,
  blessed).

## PRIMACY CLAIM
The oldest full instruction manual for the dead (c. 2600 BCE texts) — the
industry that proves humanity has always needed the transfer to be managed.

## BEST OBJECTION
- Egypt's weak link: continuity of PERSON is mostly denied — becoming-Osiris
  isn't "you continue" (it's assimilation) — the opposite of a carrier.
- The Weighted-Heart problem: it's the purest judgment-model, but judgment
  needs a judge outside the being — the self can't audit itself (set up the
  daēnā comparison from stop 4).
