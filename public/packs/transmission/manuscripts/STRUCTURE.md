# STRUCTURE — "What Actually Is Transmission?" (new film, scaffold v0.1)
> Owner brief 2026-10-20: exploring what is actually going on in diksha or
> Tibetan transmission under the hood, why it's needed for advanced practices,
> what happens if you do it without a teacher, if it's possible without
> initiation. Compiler law from day one. Status: CONCEPT + BANKED CORE.

## Working title
**What Actually Is Transmission?** (family option: *Diksha, Actually Explained*)

## Thesis
Transmission isn't a handshake or a blessing — it's a mechanism. Every
serious tradition that uses it says the same thing: some practices don't
work without a specific causal intervention. The film unpacks what that
intervention is claimed to do, why the traditions say it's necessary,
and what they say happens when you try without it.
