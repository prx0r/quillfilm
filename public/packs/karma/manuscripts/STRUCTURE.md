## Working title
**Why Karma Governs Your Life** (family option: *Karma, Actually Explained*)

## Thesis (owner's domino, kept in his order)
1. Start with the CONTROVERSIAL takes — the implications of taking karma
   seriously, and why it feels so unfair to the western rational mind.
2. That difference in views is deeply rooted in the conception of rebirth:
   one life → karma can't balance → obscene. Many lives → the books close.
3. The domino: once the mind unlocks rebirth, it unlocks karma.
4. The rational bridge: habits. Every westerner already believes choices
   groove tendencies and tendencies steer choices. Karma = that + time.
5. Fractal scaling, minimal process upward: moment → tendency → lifetime →
   across lives. Same mechanism, bigger integration window.
6. Only then: what practice IS — the process of working out karma.

## Shared spine (what the film earns)
- Karma is momentum, not justice. Justice needs a judge; momentum needs only continuity.
- The unfairness objection is valid inside one life and dissolves across lives — which is exactly why the rebirth premise loads first (domino order is load-bearing, not decorative).
- Habits are the part nobody can deny; scale is the only extrapolation.
