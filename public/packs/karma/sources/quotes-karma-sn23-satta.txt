SN 23:2 A Being (Thanissaro, dhammatalks.org — fetched 2026-10-20)

"Any desire, passion, delight, or craving for form, Radha: when one is caught up [satta] there, tied up [visatta] there, one is said to be 'a being [satta].'" [repeated for feeling, perception, fabrications, consciousness]

"Just as when boys or girls are playing with little sand castles… as long as they are not free from passion, desire, love, thirst, fever, & craving for those little sand castles, that's how long they have fun with those sand castles, enjoy them, treasure them, feel possessive of them. But when they become free… then they smash them, scatter them, demolish them with their hands or feet, and make them unfit for play. In the same way, Radha, you too should smash, scatter, & demolish form, and make it unfit for play."
