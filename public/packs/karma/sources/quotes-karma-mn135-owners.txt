MN 135 Culakammavibhanga Sutta (Thanissaro, dhammatalks.org — fetched 2026-10-20)

Subha: "what is the reason, what is the cause, why baseness & excellence are seen among human beings…? For short-lived & long-lived people are to be seen, sickly & healthy, ugly & beautiful, uninfluential & influential, poor & rich, low-born & high-born, stupid & discerning people are to be seen."

The Buddha: "Student, beings are owners of their actions, heirs of their actions, born of their actions, related through their actions, and have their actions as their arbitrator. Action is what differentiates beings in terms of baseness & excellence."

[The sutta then runs the full table — killer→short-lived, mercy→long-lived, harming→sickly, anger→ugly, envy→uninfluential, stinginess→poor, arrogance→low-born, incuriosity→stupid, and each reverse.]

Close: "So, student, the way leading to short life makes people short-lived… the way leading to discernment makes people discerning. Beings are owners of their actions, heirs of their actions, born of their actions, related through their actions, and have their actions as their arbitrator. Action is what differentiates beings in terms of baseness & excellence."
