With the breakup of the body, the wise and the foolish alike are annihilated, destroyed. They do not exist after death.’” — DN 2 Another famous annihilationist was a prince named Pāyāsi. DN 23 states that he held a materialist view similar to Ajita Kesakambalin, and that he used his power to execute criminals as an opportunity to conduct gruesome, quasi-scientific experiments to test whether any part of a human being survived death.
