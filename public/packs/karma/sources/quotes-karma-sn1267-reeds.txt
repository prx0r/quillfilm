SN 12:67 Sheaves of Reeds (Thanissaro, dhammatalks.org — fetched 2026-10-20)

"It is as if two sheaves of reeds were to stand leaning against one another. In the same way, from name-&-form as a requisite condition comes consciousness, from consciousness as a requisite condition comes name-&-form… From birth as a requisite condition, then aging-&-death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, distress, & despair come into play."

"If one were to pull away one of those sheaves of reeds, the other would fall… In the same way, from the cessation of name-&-form comes the cessation of consciousness… from the cessation of birth, then aging-&-death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, distress, & despair all cease."
