AN 6:63 Nibbedhika, kamma section (Thanissaro, dhammatalks.org — fetched 2026-10-20)

"Intention, I tell you, is kamma. Intending, one does kamma by way of body, speech, & intellect."

"And what is the cause by which kamma comes into play? Contact is the cause by which kamma comes into play."

"And what is the result of kamma? The result of kamma is of three sorts, I tell you: that which arises right here & now, that which arises later (in this lifetime), and that which arises following that."

"And what is the cessation of kamma? From the cessation of contact is the cessation of kamma; and just this noble eightfold path… is the way leading to the cessation of kamma."
