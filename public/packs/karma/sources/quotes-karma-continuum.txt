Indian Buddhist philosophers could (and did), then, coherently maintain both that ‘persons’ consist simply in causally continuous series of events, and that the series of mental events, insofar as it continues after the death of the body, has indefinite temporal extension. It is, indeed, just the post-mortem continuity of any series of mental events that is called ‘rebirth’.
