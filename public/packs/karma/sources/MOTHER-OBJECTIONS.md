## Q0. What IS the saying?
Tibetan lojong (mind-training): sevenfold cause-and-effect, step 1 —
**recognizing all beings have been my mother** → step 2 recalling their
kindness → step 3 repaying → love → compassion → bodhicitta. Lineage:
Atīśa → Chekhawa's Seven Points; Tsongkhapa's eleven-step combination.
It is step ONE of a graded technology, never a standalone belief.
Preliminary: equanimity (friend/enemy/stranger equalized) comes FIRST.

## Q1. "How is that useful? They aren't my mother now, neither of us remembers."
**Owner:** "ok so every person has been my mother, so how is that useful to
me? they arent my mother now, neither of us remember you being my mother"
**Answers, in the tradition's own words:**
- **Use = affection-generation, not information.** Serkong Rinpoche II:
  "logically it must mean that at some point… every single being has been
  our actual mother. We can't say that there is a big difference between
  the kindness of our mother in this life and… in a different life. It is
  simply a fact of remembering it or not." The mechanism needs the premise
  the way a lever needs a fulcrum — the work is the affection that follows.
- **Memory is not the criterion.** Thubten Chodron: "if somebody saved your
  life ten years ago, it would still come vividly to the mind… the fact that
  that was in the past doesn't really matter." + "we're just looking at them
  as their present life body" — the practice trains seeing PAST the present
  body, which is exactly the rebirth film's probe (wrong starting point).
- **Infant-amnesia bridge [COMPILER — not a quote]:** you don't remember
  being fed as an infant either; nobody concludes no feeding happened.
  Memory-loss is symmetric — it can't selectively cancel only past-life care.

## Q2. "Isn't this psychosis — imposing something I don't realize as true?"
**Owner:** "if i talk to someone with the knowledge that they have been my
mother i feel like some psychosis because im imposing something i dont
realise as true"
**Answers:**
- **It's explicitly provisional.** Chodron: "provisionally accepting it, so
  that we can get more of a feeling" + "Give your mind that space to play
  with it instead of seeing people as solid, fixed entities." Marx 2024
  (open access): "acting as if it were true still has an effect" even while
  doubting literal truth. Working hypothesis, not delusion — and "watch your
  mind start to fight [laughter]" names the resistance as part of the method.
- **Two-level relating (Berzin):** "we need to relate to others on a
  conventional level as they are now – as a human, a dog, a cockroach – but
  on the deeper level, we see that they all equally have Buddha nature."
  Nothing conventional is denied. Psychosis = losing the conventional; the
  practice never asks that.

## Q3. "Might as well say I was a grasshopper and he was the dog."
**Owner:** "i may as well say i was a grasshopper and this guy was the dog
that saved my life?"
**Answer: yes — and the tradition agrees.** Berzin: "No one is inherently a
human being, a cockroach, a woman, or a man… we've all taken innumerable
rebirth states." The grasshopper version is equally TRUE and equally
available — mother wins for one reason only: **leverage.** Chodron: "the
image of mother is chosen because in most cultures, the mother is the one
that people feel closest to… If you don't feel comfortable saying all
beings have been my mother, you can say father or caregiver" (Hopkins uses
best friend). The figure is INTERCHANGEABLE — which proves the practice is
strategic technology (generate gratitude), not an ontological claim about
who anybody "really" is. Grasshopper generates no gratitude; mother does.
That's the whole selection criterion, stated by the tradition itself.

## Q4. "Doesn't it complicate sex, imagining everyone as my mother?"
**Owner:** "also doesnt it complicate sex imagining everyone as my mother"
**Status: NO CANONICAL Q&A FOUND — best available assembly below; the
objection itself is film content (guard beat, owner to press a teacher).**
- **Berzin two-level (nearest thing to an answer):** relate conventionally
  "as they are now" — partner as partner — while the deeper view generates
  affection. The mother-view never issues relationship instructions.
- **Scope of "mother" in the practice:** Chodron's four kindnesses are
  body-given, protection, provision, education — the KINDNESS received, not
  the whole relationship imported. Nothing in the sequence asks you to
  reclassify anyone's present role.
- **Lojong meets you where the affliction is** (FPMT lojong notes):
  "Purify first whichever affliction is strongest… If you are more prone to
  lust… that is where you have to begin." Lust gets worked on directly, not
  routed through mother-view.
- **Order matters:** equanimity (flatten friend/enemy/stranger partiality)
  precedes mother-recognition — the sequence structurally prevents turning
  recognition into intimacy-claims on strangers.
- **Honest gap:** no teacher on record answering THIS exact form. Do not
  invent one. Flag for a recorded Q&A (Serkong Rinpoche / Chodron / HHDL
  Eight Verses teachings all take questions; search their archives first).
