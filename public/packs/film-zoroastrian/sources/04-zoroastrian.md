# STOP 4 — ZOROASTRIAN (the hinge)

## WHAT
Two-layer afterlife: individual (judgment at death) + universal (cosmic
resurrection at frashokereti). The carrier: SPLIT — the fravashi (guardian
spirit, pre-existed you, awaits you) + the daēnā (your religious soul,
appearing as a woman your deeds made beautiful or ugly).

## WHAT'S GOING ON
Death → three nights by the corpse (righteous chant the Gāthās with joy;
wicked wail, Yasna 46 — the self-recording conscience). Then the daēnā
appears (radiant maiden of fifteen for the good; ugly naked hag for the bad).
Chinvat Bridge: broad for the righteous, knife-edge for the wicked; judges
Mihr, Srōš, Rašn. At frashokereti: souls re-joined to bodies (Saošyant,
57-year process, molten-metal river ordeal), then the world renovated to
its first unfallen state.

## THE EXACT MOMENT OF DEATH
The moment = the soul rising beside the head of the corpse (Iranica
verbatim bank): "The soul of a righteous person sits during the first three
nights after death at the head of the corpse and chants the uštavaiti
Gāthās with joy… After three nights… a beautiful and radiant maiden of
fifteen years of age appears to him… she replies by identifying herself as
the daēnā, i.e., his own religious soul." The moment of death is a THREE-
NIGHT SIT — the dead person's own trial-prep period, then self-recognition.

## LOGICAL ARGUMENT (verbatim bank)
- The daēnā mechanism IS the argument: "The good thoughts, speak and deeds
  of the person while alive caused the daēnā to become more and more
  beautiful" (Encyclopaedia Iranica, Eschatology i — Shaked, free).
- Universal: bodily resurrection "attested in the Young Avesta, dated
  before the Christian era" (Timuş 2022 — the priority argument: resurrection
  was Zoroastrian before it was Christian).
- The Ahuna Vairya (Yatha Ahu Vairyo): thrice-recited at death — the oldest
  living liturgical transfer-assist (verify recitation-cite at draft).

## PRIMACY CLAIM
The hinge: Babylonian exile moves it into Judaism → judgment, angels,
last-day → Christianity. "Zoroastrian before Christian" is the historical
bull's-eye.

## BEST OBJECTION
- The split-self problem: fravashi + daēnā + soul — THREE things at the
  head of the corpse? (internal mess the buddhist stop can point at).
- The daughter-of-your-own-deeds is pure projective judgment — a medieval
  materialist would say: you're meeting your conscience, not a passenger.
