# STOP 9 — DAOIST (transformation, not transit)

## WHAT
Death = hua (transformation), the same process as seasons — Zhuangzi's
butterfly logic run to the end. Two poles on the map: (a) philosophical
Daoism — death as one more phase of the Dao's movement, "life in general
goes on, though we may not" (Philosophy Now summary bank — verify against
Zhuangzi itself at draft); (b) religious Daoism — xian immortality: the body
ITSELF is refined and carried off (the ascension) — the only school trying
to skip death entirely. Plus the third way: shijie, "corpse-deliverance" —
you fake your death; the corpse's sandals/blade/bamboo staff get found
instead of you (Penn dissertation verbatim bank).

## WHAT'S GOING ON
- Hun-po dual soul (the hun rises, the po descends — the two-soul Chinese
  model, bank exact source at draft: Liji/Yijing tradition).
- Ascension: bone-lightens, ride a dragon/celestial chariot at noon —
  no dying needed. Corpse-deliverance: escape-route death (the adepts who
  "had not enough breath, but too much flesh" — Penn dissertation, verified).
- Zhuangzi's freedom-version: if your arm becomes a rooster after death,
  use it to tell the time (the rebirth-as-mood-transfer joke — gold for the
  owner's register).

## THE EXACT MOMENT OF DEATH
Philosophical: no special moment — seasons change (you are not a project);
Religious: either ASCENSION DAY (noon, dragon, witnessed) or shijie-night
(the staged corpse). The Daoist map has the film's only "death can be
skipped by appointment" moment.

## LOGICAL ARGUMENT (verbatim bank)
- Zhuangzi's butterfly + the rooster-arm: identity as perspective, not
  inventory (bank exact Zhuangzi 18/19 passage wording at draft — RTB
  Watson/AC Graham choice matters, flag).
- The transformation cosmology (Poškaitė 2025, Problemos, free): "death as
  transformation… a general Chinese understanding" — hun-po + qi + sheng-sheng.

## PRIMACY CLAIM
The only system where the goal is to keep the SAME garment forever (anti-
garment-change!) OR to skip dying entirely — it holds BOTH "never die" and
"death is nothing" in one tradition.

## BEST OBJECTION
- The xian-track is house-holder's insurance dressed as philosophy (the
  buddhist/daoist comparativist critique: craving-for-continuation as
  the least-examined desire — pair with Gītā-obviousness).
- The Zhuangzi-mystery objection: is butterfly-you *anyone*? (the same
  satta-problem wearing a chrysalis).
