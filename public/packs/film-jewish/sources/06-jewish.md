# STOP 6 — JEWISH (gilgul + the orthodox split)

## WHAT
Two Judaisms on screen at once: (a) **mainstream rabbinic** — bodily
resurrection (teḥiyat ha-metim, Maimonides' 13th principle) + ONE life, the
soul sleeps or waits (icheidut/Sheol problem: "in Sheol, who gives you
praise?" — Ps 6:6) — an EXACT mirror of Christian absence; (b) **Kabbalistic
gilgul neshamot** — souls recycled through bodies (Bahir, Zohar, Isaac the
Blind's circular progression) — the medieval Jewish reincarnation, countable
(many trips through, e.g., 6,000 cycles popularly — cite exact kabbalistic
figure at draft), and only partially orthodox.

## WHAT'S GOING ON
Gilgul: the neshamah cycles because it still owes tikkun (repair) — the
soul's rectification-project spans lives; some souls must be reborn in
specific lineages/families (for their debts — the family-cycling doctrine).
Mainstream: after death — judgment (din), Gehinnom to purify (limited term,
twelve months max traditionally), then paradise/resurrection at the end —
the SAME zoroastrian-judgment skeleton wearing different clothes.

## THE EXACT MOMENT OF DEATH
- On the deathbed: the declaration — "Shema Yisrael… Adonai Eloheinu
  Adonai Echad" (the unifying monotheism at the last breath — famously recited
  BY RABBIS AT THE WINDOW even today), the angel of death takes the soul,
  judgment immediate.
- The dying person's moment = the soul being "drawn from the body like a
  hair from dough" (Talmud Bava Metzia 83b — Sammael pulling — verify exact
  Talmud passage wording at draft; famous image).
