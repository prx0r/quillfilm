## Śaiva title rationale (the theory, verified)
"Recognition, Not Rebirth" = the doctrine in three words: what continues is
pratyabhijñā, not transit. Backed by primary: PS 92 "he acquires another
body in accordance with his past tendencies (vāsanās)" (rebirth IS there) +
malas → "cyclical existence or saṃsāra" (Lidke) + Tantrāloka 10.294–302
**paradehapraveśa**: advanced yogis enter another body AT WILL — the only
tradition where rebirth can be done voluntarily. And the deadline-off line
(death not prerequisite) makes "You Are Not Dying" earn itself.
