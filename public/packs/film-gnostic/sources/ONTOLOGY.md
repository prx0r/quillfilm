## The jokes that write themselves
- Ancient Egypt: afterlife industry with almost no conveyor belt.
- Orthodoxy: the only stop where the animation just… turns back on.
- Yoruba: the only pre-birth self with a menu (destiny shopping).
- Daoism: the only one trying to cancel death by appointment (shijie).
- Seth vs HH: monk and trance-medium, same cut, opposite credentials.

## The thesis (global)
Everyone answers the same question; five incompatible answers; the
disagreement isn't about the evidence — it's about what kind of THING a
person is assumed to be *before* the question is asked. v1's cliffhanger
("you're at the wrong starting point") is the map's own conclusion.
