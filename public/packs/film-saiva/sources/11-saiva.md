# STOP 11 — ŚAIVA / KASHMIR (recognition)

## WHAT
The divine Self recognizes itself life to life. Īśvara-pratyabhijñā: what
continues is not a soul-subject but the cosmic act of recognition itself
operating through your jīva — samsāra is recognition-failure (you keep
NOT-recognizing your own face in the mirror of experience), liberation is
recognition-success. Body = Šiva's own instrument (deha-yātra — the body is
worship, the transmigrating self is the divine performing).

## WHAT'S GOING ON
Tattva-by-tattva mechanics: consciousness (cit) contracts into the limited
jīva-view (like a wave not knowing it's ocean), cycles through bodies as
the contraction repeats, and dissolves back at recognition — Abhinavagupta's
parināma (consciousness transforms, nothing external travels). The film's
earlier Bergson-Śaiva parallel lives HERE: extension of matter = inverted
concentration of consciousness.

meaning is inverted: at death the contraction flickers — and recognition
at THAT instant (the dīkṣā-practice trains it, mirror of tukdam) = the
wave KNOWS it's ocean and never re-contracts. Bank exact Abhinavagupta
