## Rhythm laws (specific to this film)
1. **Bell = act marker** (I/II/III/IV strikes) + merge-strike at B7. Never
   more than 4 + 1. No other percussion; no score (CINEMA rule 4).
2. **Cut density**: exposition ≤4s average · B6 montage ≤2s (the one fast
   section) · guard section hard cuts · B7/B12 ≥20s untouchable takes.
3. **Silence budget**: 3s holds ×3 (B1 title, B9 deadline-line, B10 flash) +
   5s at B12. Total ~13s — within CINEMA allowances, flagged approved.
4. **Reads vs bridges**: 24 READ blocks paced slow; bridges at talking-speed.
   Never a read and a cut on the same frame — cut between reads.
5. **The callback law**: B12 must visually rhyme B1 — same camera height,
   same mirror, mismatch now REVERSED (reflection divine from first frame,
   body plain — the film's last argument).

## Rhythm summary line (the film's feel)
slow → medium → ONE fast burst (B6) → long hold (B7) → steady deepening
(B8-B10) → hard staccato (B11) → long exhale (B12) → bell.
Sonata: statement → variation → deepest-varied-return → resolution.
