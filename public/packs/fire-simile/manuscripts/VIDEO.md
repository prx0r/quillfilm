## Thesis (candidate)

Nibbana is not a destination after death — it is what the word says: a fire
going out for lack of fuel. The film dissolves the tetralemma by showing why
"where did the fire go?" is a broken question.

## Spine (from the sermon, in order)

1. **Dabba Mallaputta** — born from a funeral pyre, arahant in the tonsure hall,
   parinibbana as self-cremation through fire-kasina: no ash, no relics, nothing left.
2. **The verses** — "as a fire burning like red-hot iron gradually subsides,
   its path is not known — so of the well-released, crossed the sensual flood,
   reached immovable bliss: no path to designate."
3. **Ghana-sanna** — compactness is the delusion: the flame looks solid like
   hot iron. The going-out of even a "solid" flame can't be traced.
4. **Upadana = fuel** — fire grasps its fuel; grasping IS fuel. Wood burns because
   of fire, fire burns because of wood (idappaccayata in one image).
5. **The tetralemma dissolved** — exists / not / both / neither after death is
   the wrong question, answered reductio-ad-absurdum via Aggivacchagotta (M I 484).
6. **The Western bugbear** — "extinction" anathema; Vedic "fire goes into hiding"
   smuggled back in; mistranslations as refuge. Say extinction plainly.
7. **Lion's roar** — parinibbuto parinibbanaya dhammam deseti: calm, teaching calm.

## Visual grammar (proposed, kinetic)

- Fire as dependent process: flame + fuel rendered as one coupled system, never
  a thing + background. The "compact" flame vs its going-out.
- The unaskable question staged and collapsed: compass/arrow searching for the
  gone fire, then the search itself dissolving.
- Palette: gochema VOID/GOLD/CRIMSON (fire = CRIMSON, going-out = VOID).
