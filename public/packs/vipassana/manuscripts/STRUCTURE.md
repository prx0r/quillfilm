## Working title
**Vipassana: What Actually Happens** (family option: *Insight, Actually Explained*)

## Thesis
"Vipassana" is the most-used and least-agreed-upon word in modern Buddhism.
Goenka says one thing, Mahasi says another, the Pali canon says a third,
and Tsongkhapa says you shouldn't start there at all. The video maps the
disagreement honestly — because the disagreement itself is the teaching.
