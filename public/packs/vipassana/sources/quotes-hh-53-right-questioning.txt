..." itself. Ask yourself: "What am I referring to? What am I designating with this word? Can I discern its actual nature?" Your theoretical, third-person knowledge is an assumption used to avoid direct insight. When you say, 'My feeling is such and such,' you must see what you are actually pointing at without the shield of abstract theory. 6 This is why, in the suttas, monks trying to understand a phenomenon would say it out loud: "Feeling, feeling, what is feeling?" Say it out loud to bring it to your mind. Then, within that enduring experience, start discerning where that feeling actually resides.
