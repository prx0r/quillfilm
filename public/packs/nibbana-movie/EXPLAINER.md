# EXPLAINER — the 6-pass explainer (independent project)

**What it is:** a standalone, layered explanation of the teaching —
six segments, told SIX times, each pass deeper than the last. Each
pass is a complete document, publishable on its own, built ONLY by
copy-paste from the excavations. It is NOT a film: no intro, no
timing, no voice, no render — those may come later for it, designed
fresh, but they do not define it. The films (A/B/C in FILMS.md) may
later adapt from it; they do not own it.

## Structure

- **The six segments** (the fixed skeleton):
  1 what nibbāna is · 2 how the mechanism works · 3 what keeps it
  running · 4 what it builds · 5 how to see through it · 6 what remains
- **Six passes** over that skeleton, each a full document:
  - **P1 — the backbone** ✅ done: 28 blocks, minimal selection that
    still makes the argument stand alone (`PASS1.md`)
  - **P1plain — the render** ✅ done: P1 block-by-block into the plain
    register (DECODE terms; fidelity checklist at its foot) — the
    pairing pattern every later pass may repeat: PASSn → PASSnplain
  - **P2 — extended**: P1 + the next tier of blocks from each segment
    pool (every remaining ### block in the excavations is a candidate),
    plus deeper blocks on P1's topics
  - **P3 — mechanism depth**: the how-blocks (anatomy of the loop,
    the stamp, the peg, contact) pulled in full
  - **P4 — practice depth**: the method-blocks in full (elements,
    signless, ratchet, endeavours, graded criteria)
  - **P5 — the defenses**: refutations, evidence, parallels added —
    every claim in P1–P4 shown against its counter-reading
  - **P6 — the assembled whole**: the complete mined treatment of all
    six segments (the excavation-level document; approaches the
    sermons themselves, still selection — the raw text remains the
    ceiling)
  (P2–P6 boundaries are tunable — nothing set in stone; the rule is
  monotonic depth, each pass = P(n-1) + more.)

## Rules (inherited, binding)

1. **Copy-paste only** — verbatim blocks from
   `53-nibbana-scaffold/sermon-NN-excavation.md`, tagged (S##).
   Nothing written, summarized, or reordered *inside* a block.
2. **Cut whole blocks** to adjust length — selection, never rewriting.
3. **READ RULE** (when any pass is voiced): only quoted text is spoken;
   everything else is card, chyron, or silence.
4. **Independent** — it does not borrow the films' intro, shells, bells
   or worlds; if it ever gains packaging, that packaging is designed
   for it.

## Assets & library (shared but not owned)

- Owns: `PASS1.md` … `PASS6.md` (future), this charter.
- Reads (shared library): the 33 excavations (source of every block),
  WEAVE (quote bank, for optional tagging), DECODE (plain-english key
  for a future render pass), VALIDATION (receipts), TEACHINGS/MAP/RAMP
  (indexes for finding blocks — tools, not content).
- Publishable unit today: **P1** — 6 segments, his words, theme-verse
  bookend. Seams on record (razor density, evidence-list, end-repeat)
  are pacing decisions for a future voice pass, each fixable only by
  cutting whole blocks.
