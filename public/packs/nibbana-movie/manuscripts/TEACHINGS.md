# TEACHINGS — the units of the film (and the .1 rule)

## The correction (this file exists because of it)

The film's units are **not sermons, not movements, not the four
truths** — the units are **the teachings themselves**: the fire
simile, dependent arising, the vortex, the path, the sandcastle, the
dog… (The sermons are the index — LESSONS/RAMP stay source-side. The
four truths are background — see COURSE's note.)

**The .1 rule:** m-unit.1 = **the minimal COMPLETE telling that lets
you say "I know what this is about."** Not a thesis fragment ("nibbāna
is extinction" doesn't teach you the fire simile) — the teaching's own
logic, compressed to its beats, in his words. Your test for a good .1:
having heard it, someone can explain the teaching back.

The ramp, per unit (not per sermon):

- **.1 minimal complete** — the teaching compressed to its beats, his
  words only, complete enough to "get it"
- **.2 claim + image** — his key quote for it + its simile
- **.3 core passage** — his prose argument on it (verbatim paragraph)
- **.4 full treatment** — ALL his passages on it, collected across the
  sittings where he treats it (excavation slices, his arrangement)
- **.5 untouched** — the raw source passages themselves, unedited

Levels 1–3 = selection only (recombine, never rewrite). Level 4 = the
curated collection (excavation material for that unit). Level 5 = raw.

## WORKED .1 EXAMPLES (the four named, assembled verbatim, raw-checked)

### teaching: the fire simile
"The truth of the matter is that the wood burns because of the fire
and the fire burns because of the wood. So it seems we already have
here a case of relatedness of this to that, idappaccayatā."
... "when a fire goes out with the exhaustion of the fuel, it is
absurd to ask in which direction the fire has gone. All that one can
say about it, is that the fire has gone out: Nibbuto tveva saṅkhaṃ
gacchati, 'it comes to be reckoned as gone out'."
"Therefore Nibbāna may be best rendered by the word 'extinction'."
[F36] (S1; full Q&A at .3–.4 from S1/S20)

### teaching: the vortex
"...an explanation of the terms saṃsāra on the one hand, and Nibbāna
on the other, that is to say 'going round', or saṃsaraṇa, and 'going
out', or nissaraṇa." (S3)
"It is a sort of whirling or turning round, as in the case of a
whirlpool or a vortex... a certain whirling round going on between
consciousness and name-and-form." (S3)
"In short, the secret of the entire saṃsāric existence is to be found
in this whirlpool." (S2)
[F10] "As is the ocean, so is the vortex now."

### teaching: dependent arising (full)
"Imasmiṃ sati idaṃ hoti, imassa uppādā idaṃ upajjati, imasmiṃ asati
idaṃ na hoti, imassa nirodhā idaṃ nirujjhati. — 'This being, this
comes to be; With the arising of this, this arises; This not being,
this does not come to be; With the cessation of this, this ceases.'"
(S26)
[F37] "This is a case of a relation of this to that, idappaccayatā."
(S1)
(.3+ = the twelve links and their application — sittings 2, 3, 26, 30)

### teaching: the path (full rundown)
"Therein, monks, right view comes first. And how, monks, does right
view come first? In one of right view, right intention arises. In one
of right intention, right speech arises... right concentration arises.
In one of right concentration, right knowledge arises. In one of right
knowledge, right deliverance arises. Thus, monks, the disciple in
higher training possessed of eight factors becomes an arahant when
possessed of the ten factors." (S32)
(.2+ = [F44] maxim "therein, depending on this, abandon this" [S30];
.3+ = seven purifications [S30/31], 36 pathways [S30], tool-kits [S31])
