# SECTIONS — what Ñāṇananda is actually saying (the binary lens)

## THE LENS

**avijjā (ignorance) = not-seeing.** Ñāṇavīra's glossary: *avijjā —
nescience (Opp. vijjā — seeing/science).* Not a thing present — a
seeing-absent. It has no discernible beginning point and it is *fed*
(AN 10.61: bad company → not hearing Dhamma → … → hindrances →
ignorance — the full food-chain of non-seeings). By definition it
obscures itself; and when seen clearly it is found never to have
existed in reality. (The "parasite" phrasing is the owner's quotation
of Ñāṇavīra — exact locus to cite at write-time; search didn't surface
it.)

**vijjā = seeing clearly = un-ignorance.** And it is **binary**: in
any moment there is either seeing or not-seeing. No third state, no
gradual substance. Nibbāna = what there is when the not-seeing stops —
not a new thing added: light isn't built, blocking is stopped.

Everything below re-reads the six scaffold sections with this lens,
plus the grab-mechanics from FORMULA. Cross-read: FORMULA = how the
grab runs · SECTIONS = what the sermons are claiming · CAMERAS = how
the traditions shoot it.

## SECTION 1 — WHAT NIBBĀNA IS

**What he's saying:** nibbāna is defined by negation (extinction,
cooling, unbinding, the un-prepared) because **the object has no
positive existence — it is the absence of the not-seeing.** The fire
image: nothing went anywhere; there's simply no more burning
[theme verse · F7 · F35]. The commentators fail precisely here —
wanting a place, an "exit from weaving," an eternal nibbāna — that is
photographing the parasite from inside and calling it real.

**The binary here:** the fire either has fuel (the not-seeing
continues) or it doesn't (seeing — the light that was available all
along). Two elements, one stop: oil running / oil out.

**The trap:** reading "what remains" as a something. Nothing is added
at nibbāna. You don't build the light; you stop blocking it.

## SECTION 2 — HOW THE MECHANISM WORKS

**What he's saying:** the mechanism (this → then → that) is invisible
only *from inside it* — **avijjā is the non-inspection of the law**.
Dependent origination is the parasite's metabolic cycle — and AN
10.61's food-chain is its menu. See the mechanism once and it flips
reality-status: what was "a real world arising" becomes "preparations
arising" (saṅkhāra). Nothing physical changes. The vortex: the
not-seeing *is* the whirl; the ocean was always there — "as is the
ocean, so is the vortex now" [F10].

**The binary here:** same water — whirling or not. The world either
seems solid (not-seen) or is seen as preparations (seen). One switch.

**The trap:** thinking you must stop the mechanism. The mechanism
keeps running; it stops being *believed*. Reality doesn't change
status by changing content.

## SECTION 3 — WHAT KEEPS IT RUNNING

**What he's saying:** the food supply. The parasite is fed by every
unwatched stamp (saññā accepted as the thing), every spiral
(papañca — the stamp extending itself), and the peg (asmimāna — the
not-seer installed as the seer). Grammar does the installation behind
your back: the "am" peg makes "I"-thinking the default without your
seeing it happen. The long lists in the tradition (kileasas,
hindrances) are not separate problems — **they are the food inventory**
(the AN 10.61 cascade, itemized).

**The binary here:** each moment is either a feeding or a cut.
Attention or its absence. That's all the fuel the parasite ever gets.

**The trap:** treating the lists as many enemies. There is one
parasite with a menu.

## SECTION 4 — WHAT THE MECHANISM BUILDS

**What he's saying:** the parasite's architecture — a two-sided world
(me / not-me) and an **observer: the not-seer posing as the seer.**
This is its central nest. Nāṇananda's move is to look for the seer
directly — and it is not found (the dog: it is *seeing because it is
looking* [F12]; the puppets walk with no puppeteer [F32]). Not-found =
the nest revealed as empty.

**The binary here:** either the observer is seen as made (nest empty)
or it remains the unseen real (parasite secure). This is the deepest
cut in the six — everything in §4 exists to set it up.

**The trap:** "but who is looking?" — the parasite's defense reflex.
It can only be answered by looking at the looking itself.

## SECTION 5 — HOW TO SEE THROUGH IT

**What he's saying:** the binary operation, performed deliberately.
Yoniso manasikāra = look at the source, not the object. Every
yathābhūtaṃ (see-X-as-X: elements as elements, signs as signs,
feelings as feelings) = switching the light on at one more spot. The
razor [F15]: gentle inspection — **force is just another grab; the
parasite eats grabs and survives your war on it.** Māgandiya's line
stands as the rule: not by views, nor by their absence — the binary
isn't held, it's operated. Each seen-and-unreinforced instance is one
feeding cut (the grab → nothing practice; extinction is trial-based).

**The binary here:** seeing the grab as a grab vs. being inside it.
There is no third position — which is why "neither by views nor
without views" is the correct answer to every view-question.

**The trap:** trying to destroy ignorance. You cannot grab your way
out of not-seeing. The practice is only: stop not-seeing.

## SECTION 6 — WHAT REMAINS

**What he's saying:** the parasite had no positive existence — so
when the light comes, **nothing is subtracted and nothing added:**
the world "just stands there" [AVI 63] — reality *un-ignored*, which
was the default all along. Un-ignorance is not an achievement; it's
the removal of a block. The two elements = the same absence at two
fuel-levels. The lotus, the ocean, the fire gone out: ordinary,
unenhanced, exact — reality with the not-seeing absent.

**The binary, closed:** there is seeing and there is not-seeing.
Nibbāna is the first, with nothing added. Saṃsāra is the second, with
everything added.

## THE ONE-PARAGRAPH VERSION (film-ready)

The whole of Buddhism says: you are not seeing, and the not-seeing
feels like reality. When you see — instantly, completely, in that
moment — there is nothing to remove, because there was never
anything there but the not-seeing. That is nibbāna: not a new state,
the light that was always available. The grab keeps running only while
it's unseen; see it, once, in every costume it owns, and the costume
factory loses power. The traditions each point a camera at this one
event. The 33 sermons are the ID list of the costumes.
