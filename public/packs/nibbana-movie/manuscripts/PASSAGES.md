## P4 · I11–I14 — the arrival and the screen (the spectator builds)
Voice enters on the walk:
[F31] "The worldling discerns the world around him with the help of six
narrow beams of light, namely the six sense-bases."
— pixels resolve; the trees; the screen —
[F1] "…a film show or drama is produced, in the last analysis, by the
spectator himself."
[F26] "In fact, what we have here is only a heap of imaginings."
— pixels increase until the picture is pure pixels —
[F33] "While keeping one's postures in this manner, one sees in outline
one's own form as if one were acting in a monodrama." (ALT: the walk
itself is the monodrama)
[F3] "The rapidity itself gives an impression of permanence, which is a
perversion, vipallāsa."
(Sequence logic: what the eye is → who makes the show → what the
"show" is made of → the form of the self as performance → why it
nonetheless looks solid.)
