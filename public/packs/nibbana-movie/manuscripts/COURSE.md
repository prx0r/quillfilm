# COURSE — how to structure the 33 sermons (Film A, structure of record)

## The finding (from the texts, not imposed)

Opening-line analysis: **22+ of 33 sermons explicitly continue the
previous one** ("in our last sermon...", "the other day..."). The
series is therefore NOT 33 independent modules and NOT six boxes of
material — it is **one continuous argument, cut into 33 sittings**,
with exactly one pivot the author declares himself (S27: purgative →
elixir). The structure the content hands us:

1. **Follow the chain.** Sermon order = argument order. Re-sorting into
   the 6 sections is a *retrieval* operation (the excavations already
   did it); it is not the presentation structure.
2. **Cut at the natural joints.** Five movements where the argument
   changes gear — sizes fall where the content puts them, not evenly.
3. **One pivot, declared** (S27|S28 boundary — the author's own words).

## THE FIVE MOVEMENTS (= Film A's acts)

**M1 · THE OBJECT (sermons 1–8, 8 sittings)**
What nibbāna is and why the question is hard: theme verse, fire, nama
is name, dependent origination, the tangle — then the show is
introduced (the cinema, the razor, the five similes, the dog on the
plank, the puppet). Establishes: the object + that experience is a show.
Leans on: sections 1, 2 (with 4 seeded).

**M2 · THE MACHINERY (sermons 9–17, 9 sittings)**
How the show runs: gem → contact → mirror → papanca/nippapanca →
vortex → Bahiya's instruction → the six beams. The fat center of
section 2 material. Establishes: how experience is fabricated and the
first seeing-through tools.
Leans on: sections 2, 3 (with 5 seeded).

**M3 · THE CENTRAL ARGUMENT (sermons 18–25, 8 sittings)**
The single longest unbroken chain in the series: lotus → oil lamp →
fire catechism (20) → Anurādha & the sandcastles (21) → Yamaka & the
murderer (22) → Rohitassa's world-end (23) → Māra's jurisdiction &
the dyad (24) → Kāḷakārāma's planes (25). One argument: what nibbāna
is, defended against every misread (the tetralemma arc runs through
all eight sittings). Establishes: section 1 completed + sections 4/6
seeded.
NOTE: this is 24% of the series devoted to ONE chain — the spine.

**M4 · THE HINGE (sermons 26–27, 2 sittings)**
The magic-show parable (the spectator thesis in full) and the chess →
**the author's own pivot**: "Up to now, administering a purgative... it
is time now for the elixir." The film's intermission; the trojan's
home.
Leans on: sections 2, 3 (the spectator = section 4 seed).

**M5 · THE PATH (sermons 28–33, 6 sittings)**
The elixir, in order: element meditation → the 36 pathways → the
signless & the planing → the seven tool-kits → middle-path depth →
the synopsis ("all of it = the two ends and the middle").
Leans on: sections 5, 6 (the payoff seats — where segment 6 finally
gets fed properly).

## How the other structures fit INSIDE this one

- **The 6 sections** = recurring checkpoint questions asked inside every
  movement (the spiral the sermons themselves perform — each movement
  touches all six; the excavations are pre-sorted so the material is
  findable). Retrieval + rhythm, not acts.
- **The 4 truths** = the skeleton stated where Nāṇananda states it (M5)
  and carried silently behind M3–M4; FORMULA's L7 is its compression.
- **The levels of detail (L1–L6)** = the *depth dial* applied per
  movement: any movement can be told compressed (L1) or receipted (L6);
  the film chooses one depth per act rather than building 36 units.
- **The invisibility/urgency doctrine** = M2's interpretive key (why
  the machinery is unseen) and M4's device (the film never shows a
  defect).
- **Film B (cinematic)** is untouched by this: it already has its own
  structure (hook → prologue → zones → coda) and mirrors these five
  acts loosely through its zone walk.

## Why not the alternatives (settled)

- **Pure6-box re-sort:** splits single sermons across acts; no dukkha
  seat; dual-homing chaos. It's a library index, not a story.
- **Forced even6 movements:** symmetry reflex — the content's joints
  are 8/9/8/2/6, and the 2-sitting hinge is real (the pivot deserves
  its own beat).
- **Level-structure as the top frame:** depth is a property of telling,
  not of ordering — ordering must come from the source.
