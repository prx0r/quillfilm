# COURSE — how to structure the 33 sermons (Film A, structure of record)

## The finding (from the texts, not imposed)

Opening-line analysis: **22+ of 33 sermons explicitly continue the
previous one** ("in our last sermon...", "the other day..."). The
series is therefore NOT 33 independent modules and NOT six boxes of
material — it is **one continuous argument, cut into 33 sittings**,
with exactly one pivot the author declares himself (S27: purgative →
elixir). The structure the content hands us:

1. **Follow the chain.** Sermon order = argument order. Re-sorting into
   the 6 sections is a *retrieval* operation (the excavations already
   did it); it is not the presentation structure.
2. **Cut at the natural joints.** Five movements where the argument
   changes gear — sizes fall where the content puts them, not evenly.
3. **One pivot, declared** (S27|S28 boundary — the author's own words).

## THE FIVE MOVEMENTS (= Film A's acts)

**M1 · THE OBJECT (sermons 1–8, 8 sittings)**
What nibbāna is and why the question is hard: theme verse, fire, nama
is name, dependent origination, the tangle — then the show is
introduced (the cinema, the razor, the five similes, the dog on the
plank, the puppet). Establishes: the object + that experience is a show.
Leans on: sections 1, 2 (with 4 seeded).

**M2 · THE MACHINERY (sermons 9–17, 9 sittings)**
How the show runs: gem → contact → mirror → papanca/nippapanca →
vortex → Bahiya's instruction → the six beams. The fat center of
section 2 material. Establishes: how experience is fabricated and the
first seeing-through tools.
Leans on: sections 2, 3 (with 5 seeded).

**M3 · THE CENTRAL ARGUMENT (sermons 18–25, 8 sittings)**
The single longest unbroken chain in the series: lotus → oil lamp →
fire catechism (20) → Anurādha & the sandcastles (21) → Yamaka & the
murderer (22) → Rohitassa's world-end (23) → Māra's jurisdiction &
the dyad (24) → Kāḷakārāma's planes (25). One argument: what nibbāna
is, defended against every misread (the tetralemma arc runs through
all eight sittings). Establishes: section 1 completed + sections 4/6
seeded.
NOTE: this is 24% of the series devoted to ONE chain — the spine.

**M4 · THE HINGE (sermons 26–27, 2 sittings)**
The magic-show parable (the spectator thesis in full) and the chess →
**the author's own pivot**: "Up to now, administering a purgative... it
is time now for the elixir." The film's intermission; the trojan's
home.
Leans on: sections 2, 3 (the spectator = section 4 seed).

**M5 · THE PATH (sermons 28–33, 6 sittings)**
The elixir, in order: element meditation → the 36 pathways → the
signless & the planing → the seven tool-kits → middle-path depth →
the synopsis ("all of it = the two ends and the middle").
Leans on: sections 5, 6 (the payoff seats — where segment 6 finally
gets fed properly).

## THE BACKBONE — the four truths (declared)

The argument skeleton is the four noble truths (the author's own frame:
S28 opens the elixir with "right view is defined as the knowledge of
all the four noble truths"; FORMULA's L7 is its one-sentence
compression). The six segments are the chapters; these are the spine:

| truth | in plain (our decode) | seats | representative sermons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 dukkha** — suffering | the grab's discrepancy: the sting + "my sting"; the constructed carrying-burden | segment 4 (what it builds) + INVISIBILITY | 10, 13, 14, 16, 17 |
| **T2 samudaya** — origin | the grab itself: mechanism + fuel (the stamp, the spiral, the peg, the feeding) | segments 2 + 3 | 2, 3, 5, 6, 9–17 |
| **T3 nirodha** — cessation | the grabbing stops — definition (before) and realization (after): the two elements, one stop | segments 1 + 6 | 1, 7, 18–22, 25, 26 |
| **T4 magga** — path | watching it end: attention at the source, the ratchet of 36 pathways, tool-kits, middle depth | segment 5 + the elixir half | 4, 5, 8, 14, 15, 28–33 |

Rules: the four truths state the argument; the six segments organize the
delivery; the 33 sittings are the index (LESSONS.md); any quote lives
in exactly one segment but may serve any truth — dual membership is
tagged, never duplicated.

## How the other structures fit INSIDE this one

- **The 6 sections** = recurring checkpoint questions asked inside every
  pass (the spiral the sermons themselves perform — each pass touches
  all six; the excavations are pre-sorted so material is findable).
  Retrieval + rhythm, not acts.
- **The 4 truths** = the skeleton stated where Nāṇananda states it (M5)
  and carried silently behind M3–M4; FORMULA's L7 is its compression.
- **The levels of detail (L1–L6)** = the depth vocabulary for each
  PASS: P1=L1 · P2=L2 · P3=L3 · P4=L4–L6 · P5=L6-still (see THE LOOP).
- **The invisibility/urgency doctrine** = M2's interpretive key (why
  the machinery is unseen) and M4's device (the film never shows a
  defect).
- **Film B (cinematic)** is untouched: own structure (hook → prologue →
  zones → coda), loosely mirroring these five acts through its zone walk.

## THE LOOP (same thing, five times, expanded)

Not a ramp along the sequence — **a loop over it.** The same course is
told FIVE times. Each telling restarts at the SAME starting point and
re-walks the same sequence, expanded one level. Units are named
`m<pass>.<step>` (m1.1, m1.2, m1.3, m1.4 ...).

- **Pass 1 (m1.x): the claim.** Each unit = one statement + one image.
  Fast, wide, sparse.
- **Pass 2 (m2.x): claim + evidence.** Same restart; each unit now
  carries its banked quote and simile (the 33 sittings become the
  index here — any unit can drop to the sitting with its best line).
- **Pass 3 (m3.x): mechanism.** Same restart — how it works inside:
  loop anatomy, the stamp, the practice mechanics (grab.html anatomy
  lives at this depth).
- **Pass 4 (m4.x): THE FULL ENCAPSULATION.** By the fourth telling of
  any unit everything is on the table — one position at full depth
  contains the whole (the hologram property). m1.4 = first full
  encapsulation.
- **Pass 5 (m5.x): what remains.** Nothing new. The same units, finally
  quiet — the film's own "section 6": the all-there, stated once,
  still.

Property: restart → expand → restart → expand → by .4 it is all there →
.5 is silence with the all-there. The audience learns the spine by
repetition and receives only *depth*, never a new plot.

VISUALIZATION: each pass is a ZOOM into the same frame. m1.1 = the
simple icon; m1.4 = the same icon fully exploded and interlocked (the
motif ecology complete); m5 = the icon, still, unannotated. Canvas
native: same objects, expanding — the quill-canvas/PREZI motion from
the vision doc. Motifs never change identity between passes — only
resolution (leitmotif rule preserved). Frame density also follows the
walk within each pass: M1 zones sparse, M3 zones dense, M5 fine.

## Why not the alternatives (settled)

- **Pure6-box re-sort:** splits single sermons across acts; no dukkha
  seat; dual-homing chaos. It's a library index, not a story.
- **Forced even6 movements:** symmetry reflex — the content's joints
  are 8/9/8/2/6, and the 2-sitting hinge is real (the pivot deserves
  its own beat).
- **Level-structure as the top frame:** depth is a property of telling,
  not of ordering — ordering must come from the source.
- **Monotonic position-gradient** (depth rising once, along 1→33):
  settled against — depth is delivered by RESTARTING and expanding
  (the loop), not by passing once faster then slower.
