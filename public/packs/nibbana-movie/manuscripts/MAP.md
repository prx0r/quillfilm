# MAP — the minimal map (his words only · we write nothing)

THE RULE: **recombine, never rewrite.** No new sentences, no
summaries, no our-words — only verbatim units, moved into order.

Rules: every line in quotes is Ñāṇananda's, verified against the raw
sermons this session. `[F#]` = banked in WEAVE; `(S#)` = excavation-
verified but not yet banked (bank before film use). Everything else in
the map is a structural label, not prose. Plain-english swap comes
AFTER the mapping, never before.

**m1.2 THE MACHINERY (9–17)**
[F31] "The worldling discerns the world around him with the help of

**m1.3 THE ARGUMENT (18–25)**
[F35] "For, the destruction of craving, Rādha, is Nibbāna."
