# FILMS — the two-film split (ownership map)

Three layers exist; two of them are films. Everything else is library.

```
LIBRARY (shared, never a film)
  53-excavations (satsang-extensions) — the mined content: 33 sermons,
    6-section sorted, Voice banks, Pass2/Pass3
  WEAVE.md    — [F1]–[F35] verbatim bank (both films quote from it)
  DECODE.md   — plain-English glossary + THE JOURNEY (both films speak it)
  VALIDATION.md — canon/science receipts (both films claim only what's validated)

FILM A — THE CLEAR ONE (ruthlessly clear reasoning; no trojan)
  the line of reasoning from this session, written down and filmed
  spine:   FORMULA.md   — the owner's chain, continued; substitutions;
                          stream entry; grab→nothing sequence (G1–G4);
                          same-stop-many-cameras
  content: SECTIONS.md  — the six sections re-read under the
                          avijjā/vijjā binary + the invisibility law
  visuals: grab.html    — the realtime/slow-mo/practice model (G1–G4
                          rendered from this exact logic)
  rules:   may NAMING directly (it explains); plain language only;
           may say "nibbāna" at L2 of the chain; no metaphor-for-its-
           own-sake — every image must carry the inference

FILM B — THE CINEMATIC (the pointer / magic show; the specced one)
  INTRO.md      — the owner's intro, verbatim (locked)
  MOVIE.md      — thesis, 4 laws, THE POINTER/trojan, cinematography
  ARC.md        — spectator's comprehension map, two pennies, confusion
                  budget
  SCREENPLAY.md — scene blocks (hook → prologue → 6 sections as zones →
                  coda), rhythm, bells
  STORYBOARD.md — the board of record: 30 rows (H/I/Z/E), timings,
                  narration IDs, bells
  PASSAGES.md   — sequenced quote-blocks per scene
  world:   MOTIFS.md (51-nibbana-movie) — zones ↔ sections ↔ bells
  rules:   trojan (never announce); narrator = Ñāṇananda's words only;
           never show a defect (invisibility); loop to H1; bells at
           motif introductions only
```

**How they relate:** Film A is the *argument*; Film B is the *same
argument with its mouth closed*. Film A may quote the chain out loud;
Film B only performs it (that's the trojan). They share one bank, one
glossary, one validation layer — so every line in either film can be
traced to sermon + plain-English + receipt. Changes to WEAVE/DECODE/
VALIDATION propagate to both; changes to ARC/SCREENPLAY/STORYBOARD are
Film B only; changes to FORMULA/SECTIONS are Film A only.
