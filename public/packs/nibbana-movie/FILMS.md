**How they relate:** Film A is *the argument, told out loud, from
every chair in the room*; Film B is the same argument with its mouth
closed, hidden in a magic show. Film A says "nibbāna is the end of
grabbing" and then proves it seven times from seven traditions; Film B
never says it once — it rings bells and lets you find it. Film A's
camera-tour and Film B's zone-tour are deliberate mirrors: one walks
the *teachers*, the other walks the *images*. They share one bank, one
glossary, one validation layer — so every line in either film can be
traced to sermon + plain-English + receipt. Changes to WEAVE/DECODE/
VALIDATION propagate to both; changes to ARC/SCREENPLAY/STORYBOARD are
Film B only; changes to FORMULA/SECTIONS are Film A only.
