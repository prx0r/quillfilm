# INVISIBILITY — the definition (the thing we keep circling)

## THE DEFINITION

**Ignorance (avijjā) is not a thing inside your experience — it is a
structural feature of how experience is generated: the model that
constructs your world is transparent while running. You look *through*
it, never *at* it. So the construction appears as *given*.**

**Ignorance = the appearing-as-given.** The trick cannot appear *as* a
trick inside the medium it produces — that is not an accident, it is
the operating mode.

## DECOMPOSITION (the parts, plain)

**1. What you take as given — the world, others, the pull.**
The world you meet (objects, persons, the feel of toward/away) is
mirage-structured: every component real, the water is the model's
offer. Desert floor + eyes + water-prediction = mirage: nothing is
lying to you; the *pull* is added by the system that needs you to
move. Same for others: the person you meet is your rendering — labels,
categories, projections running — (ethical line: the rendering is
constructed, **their pain is not** — process-realism is not denial of
other minds; the flux-pain is exactly as real as your flux).

**2. Why structural, not accidental.**
Perception must PRESENT its best guess as reality to guide action —
fitness favors presentation-as-given (a labeled-as-model perception
would paralyze: you'd re-decide what's real before every act).
Ignorance is therefore the system running correctly, not malfunctioning.
Selection built the transparency; inspection is the extra move nothing
selected for.

**3. The zoom — "a someone being said."**
Zoom into the process of *someone-ing*: the attributions run
(experiencing, preferring, claiming). Look for the someone being said:
there is sad-ing, no sad-one; wanting, no wanter. **You're not a
someone that's sad — you're just not anyone at all.** (Metzinger:
being no one; the self-model with no one inside it.) The correct
reaction is not grief: nothing was lost — *there was no one to lose.*
The sadness and the incredible are the same fact seen twice: the
process is real, the owner was the overlay.

**4. Why it nevertheless betrays itself** (from SECTIONS):
- **Symptoms:** the grab's failures — wanting-without-liking; the
  promise that never arrives. Suffering is the visible trace of the
  invisible.
- **Glitches:** the mirror double-take; the gap that never closes by
  running; change blindness (lab proof: people miss massive changes
  while staring — missing something and sure they're not).
- **Outside signal:** parato ghoso — an exit code cannot be generated
  from inside the run (AN 10.61's food-chain runs both ways: not-
  hearing feeds ignorance; hearing feeds clear knowing).

## THE SCOPE (why the doctrine stops where it stops)

**The goal is the removal of what is urgent — nothing more.** Once the
suffering-generation stops, nothing else is urgent; the rest of reality
can stand unexamined ("the various things just stand there"). This is
why the canon brackets whole categories of questions: they "do not lead
to disenchantment, to dispassion, to cessation" — set aside, not
answered. This is why Ñāṇananda chose the theme verse "to help create
the correct atmosphere of meditative attention rather than dealing with
deep suttas in academic isolation" (S1). This is why his research
method is gratification → danger → stepping out (S28) — pragmatic
brackets, not metaphysics-for-its-own-sake.

**A philosophy scoped to the urgent:** investigate exactly what
generates the urgent (the grab chain); leave the rest to stand.
Curiosity-about-reality-for-its-own-sake is itself a lean — the
system grabbing at the total picture. It is not forbidden; it is
*unfunded*. No urgency rides on it.

## THE RECEIPTS (what supports this definition)

- **Metzinger:** phenomenal transparency — the self-model worn like
  red glasses; making it opaque = the practice. (Formal mechanism.)
- **Change blindness / inattentional blindness:** missing what you're
  staring at, certain you're not missing it. (Lab proof.)
- **Kashiwagi et al. (Science 2025):** thalamic activity precedes the
  flash — *consciousness begins with the brain's preparation to see,
  not the seen* — the movie runs before the frame. (Gate priority.)
- **Uchiyama et al. (Eur J Neurosci, Oct 2026):** a physical flicker
  can drive cortex and never reach awareness — processed yet not
  admitted; conscious flicker = beta→gamma integration at ~300ms.
  (The gate itself, measured.)
- **Ihalainen, Oswald, Laureys et al. (arXiv:2610.20856, 14 Oct
  2026):** "Awakening as a Trait-Level Transformation of Consciousness"
  — proposes the **enactive-dereification model**: awakening = a
  *dereification gradient* from reified → relational experience,
  "a reduction in identification with mental contents rather than the
  acquisition of new content." **Neuroscience formalizing exactly
  this definition one week ago:** reified = appearing-as-given;
  dereified = the definition removed; no new content — subtraction.
- **Canon:** ignorance has no beginning and is fed (AN 10.61); with
  the abandoning of one thing ignorance is abandoned and clear knowing
  arises — ignorance itself (SN 35.80); avijjā = nescience, opp.
  vijjā (Ñāṇavīra glossary).
- **The sermons:** "what we have here is only a heap of imaginings"
  (S33); the spectator makes the show (F1/F2); the dog seeing because
  it is looking (F12); "the various things just stand there" (AVI 63).

## OPEN FLAGS (not part of the definition)

- Whether the base reality under the flux is "information" (Wheeler/
  IIT) is **undecided** — our definition needs only that experience is
  constructed, not what the substrate is.
- The other-minds line (§1) is a boundary marker: construction of the
  other ≠ non-reality of their suffering. Never let "it's a mirage"
  be said about another's pain.
- The EDM paper is a preprint (arXiv, Oct 2026) — cite as preprint.

## WHERE THIS LIVES

The governing definition for SECTIONS (the lens), FORMULA (L5's
invisibility), ARC (the film's device), and the films' hard rule: **the
defect can never be shown — only the voice from outside can point at
it, and only the symptoms can stage it.**
