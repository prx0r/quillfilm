## Working title
**How to Actually Achieve Shamatha** (family option: *Calm Abiding, Actually Explained*)

## Thesis
Shamatha isn't a technique — it's a trajectory. The elephant-pathway metaphor
(Wallace) shows what's actually happening: attention starts wild, gets
tired, and the practitioner doesn't chase it but waits it out. The video
walks the full path: why shamatha comes before vipassana, what the different
traditions actually mean by it, and what happens when it's done.
