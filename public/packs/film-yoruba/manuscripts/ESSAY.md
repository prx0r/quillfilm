the only map where you pick yourself before you arrive. at the crossroads,
before birth: you choose your ori — your head, your destiny. a menu. no
other stop has an agent choosing pre-birth; most don't even have a
"pre-birth."

and the returns are operational: the àbíkú — the spirit-children who cycle,
dying and returning until the community breaks the loop: anklets tied,
markings made — the town runs experiments on the reincarnator. the folk
equivalent of the forest monks' past-life checks: [CALLBACK v1] verification
by practical test, in both cases.

and the dead keep civic rights: egungun — the ancestors dance in costume
among the living, yearly. transfer as parade.

says who: the community verifies, not the doctrine. the objection: colonial
dismissal of abiku as superstition runs into modern anthropology — bank the
pushback at draft. and internally: if you chose your head, whose fault is
suffering?
